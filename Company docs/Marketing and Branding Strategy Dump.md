## MB-0005 — Guarantee and accountability

### USER INPUT

> Yes we need to come up with a way to guarantee something more than just structure of course ethar is there as a qualifier and may even become something of a usp with a little polishing and proper marketing, but the real credibility and accountability comes from being able to guarantee something. The only thing is we cannot guarantee how human being will behave after a training, like you buy a software update and know this does a particular action better, you can't buy a training with that mind set because humans are not uniform, that is in fact the reason most small business or small minded businesses don't want to invest in training no matter how much they need it.

### SYNTHESIS

The distinction introduced here is between guaranteeing **human outcomes** and guaranteeing something that the training provider can actually define and assess. Humans are not uniform like software, so the guarantee must attach to a demonstrable aspect of learning rather than to all future behaviour.

## MB-0006 — Course buy-back / Mastery Guarantee

### USER INPUT

> Learn it, become so good that if you are able to teach us the course the same way as us and fitting to our parameters we will give you your fees back.

### ASSISTANT RESPONSE

Proposed formulation: **The Vimuktam Mastery Guarantee** — learn it, demonstrate it, teach it, get your fee back.

The proposed distinction was:

> We don't refund you for completing our course. We refund you for proving that you have mastered it.

A possible pathway was:

**Student → Mastery → Certified practitioner → Trainer → Vimuktam training community**

### SYNTHESIS

A selected Vimuktam course could potentially have a fee-back mechanism based on demonstrated mastery: the participant must teach the course in their own delivery while remaining faithful to Vimuktam's parameters and demonstrate the required competencies.

This remains a candidate mechanism requiring legal and commercial validation.

## MB-0008 — Shimla Tattu: separate marketing talking point

### USER INPUT

> Note on small minded businesses: the analogy of a tattu in shimla, how the small minded owner would beat it when he is tired of boredom and stress but not give him love and rest that would increase his life and would actually be benifitial to his business ultimately. Because while the logic is plain sense it comes across as philosophy and hence is not comprehended by the small minded tattu wala. (This is an extremely important talking point keep it seprate, we will create a list of these things) Create this in the marketing strategy idea list)

### SYNTHESIS

This remains a separate marketing talking point. The example illustrates a communication problem: a practice may be plainly beneficial in practical terms, yet fail to persuade when presented at an abstract or philosophical level that the intended audience does not comprehend.

The underlying marketing lesson is to translate Vimuktam principles into concrete language, incentives and consequences without losing the principle itself.

## MB-0011 — Sponsored subsidies and scholarships

### USER INPUT

> Another long term marketing strategy is to try to get sponsored subsidies and scholarships for Vimuktam courses, because we are so loved by researchers and scholars

### ASSISTANT RESPONSE

Potential sources discussed included academic institutions and research bodies, foundations/philanthropic organisations, corporate CSR, cultural institutions, individual patrons, successful Vimuktam alumni and eventually research grants.

A potential loop was identified:

**Scholarly credibility → sponsorship → accessibility → more participants → more evidence/research → greater scholarly credibility**

### SYNTHESIS

Access through sponsorship is a possible long-term strategic pillar rather than merely a discount mechanism. The underlying idea is to use scholarly and institutional credibility to increase access to Vimuktam education.

## MB-0012 — Vimuktam Sponsorship Facilitation

### USER INPUT

> 1. We help you select a course from Vimuktam's repertoire and customised needed, then we help you network and find leads some your some ours and we help you develop a pitch and present it with you. If you get a sponsorship we get paid, if you don't no hard feelings.

### SYNTHESIS

The proposed model:

1. Help the participant select and customise a Vimuktam course.
2. Help identify sponsorship leads — some supplied by the participant and some by Vimuktam.
3. Help develop the sponsorship pitch.
4. Present the pitch together.
5. Vimuktam is paid if sponsorship succeeds; if it does not, there is no hard feeling / no equivalent success fee.

Possible principle:

> **If we believe your education deserves funding, we'll help you find someone willing to fund it.**

## MB-0013 — Vimuktam School: Mehenga School, Saste Mein

### USER INPUT

> 2. Mehenga school saste mein, expensive schools teach excellent things, but not everyone can afford those. The Vimuktam School offers complimentary courses at reasonable prices so that the children can get their academics from wherever the extra curriculars that expensive schools provide we can give to the children.

### SYNTHESIS

Vimuktam School is conceived as an enrichment ecosystem rather than a replacement for academic schooling.

The proposition discussed was:

> **Get your academics wherever you get them. Get the enrichment here.**

Possible positioning:

> **The enrichment of an expensive school, without the expensive school.**

The original phrase **“Mehenga School, Saste Mein”** is retained as an idea label.
