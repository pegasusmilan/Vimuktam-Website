## PO-0001 — Vimuktam Debate: practice, experiment and rejecting false binaries

> No, this model of adversarial symmetry as interpreted by the British colonial administration, is not fucking excellent. That is in fact what is wrong with the whole bloody residue of a world ruled by Britain. That is precisely why we have a fucked up media, corrupt and unjust judiciary, and an absolute monster of a pandemic of boredom.

This is the context for the rejection of inherited adversarial/Oxford-style debate as the default model of inquiry.

## PO-0002 — Practice can make debate better

> practicing and idea will make you good at debate too, but this time it will be way better than without practice. You will choose proper ideas to debate and stay quite and content within yourself, if you have nothing to add to a debate.
>
> Some ideas for VIC can be wheather seperating wet and dry waste is helpful for the environment, or even working together to find out if non vegetarian food in fact is more burdensome to the planet, rather than dry debating "school uniforms a bane or boon?" The underlying idea: debate should become an empirical and practical exercise. Ideas worthy of debate are those that can be **engaged with and Practiced**, rather than merely placed into artificial “bane or boon” binaries.

## PO-0003 — Lofty subjects can also become Practice

> Another thing is we can convert anything into practice through this. Like now lofty subject is out of reach. In fact the loftier the devate, the more abstract the more it is important to fix it through this method. Was Gandhi a good guy? Was michael jackson a pedo? Is one kardashian better than the other? Interstellar is better or inception? Any idea that can be Engaged with, (another way of defining Practice) is worthy of debating, anything that isn't can be accepted as a matter of choice.

Core principle: **Practice is a way of making even abstract or lofty questions concrete enough to engage with.** Where a proposition cannot meaningfully be engaged with, it may be a matter of personal choice rather than something requiring victory in argument.

## PO-0004 — Friends, disagreement and acceptance

> Friends can argue still by the way, Vimuktam doesn't ask for people to be serious all the bloody time. It only says to be more accepting of another's choice when it doesn't impact you. This if anything will make people come closer make them more loving

> Like that story of the first man to have discovered the stone that looked like a face, it is fun to observe how humans must have figured out not just mental but real physical things in the environment and used their intelligence as a reflex to the sense of the self and created newer and newer waysto cater to the self. ## PO-0006 — Science explains how; Vimuktam asks why

The recurring distinction developed here is between:

- science as an increasingly precise account of **how** phenomena work;
- contemplation as an inquiry into **why there is an experiencer, what the experiencer wants, and how the experiencer chooses**.

The conscious self is proposed as the point from which scientific inquiry itself becomes intelligible: someone observes, wants, chooses, experiments and remembers.

> Vimuktam is the Science of Contemplation. For Humans Action is involuntary, reactive and reflexive. It is the Contemplation that decides how far, how better, how lasting an action is. Humans are the only ones that can contemplate before taking an action. All animals tonsome extent, but humans have this ability at the most. We have the DLPFC. Vimuktam is the arithmatization, ganan, of this power of Contemplation.

Key concept: **Vimuktam = Science of Contemplation**, with “ganan”/arithmetization referring to the systematic measurement, practice and development of contemplation.

> -Planning

> -Being Still in the now.
>
> Problems arise when the gears are sort of lose. So they Just keep shifting on their own, while the body may or may not be in line with current gear of the mind.
>

>
> This main gear is in fact the one that teaches us how to use the other two, which with enough practice do we realise, are just variations of this default state of being of the soul.
>

The three gears are **Planning, Acting, and Being Still in the Now**. Stillness is treated as the foundational Practice through which the other two modes become governable.

## PO-0012 — Core structure of a Vimuktam Debate

> The core structure of a Vimuktam Debate. Two or more sides have a pre-declared specific preference towards a concept of philosophical importance (Vimuktam Things)
> -they both want to take something useful out of this debate that can help them have a better experience with the VT in question.
>
> -it is already assumed that all or both parties are deserving of respect, they do not want to win the argument by proving themselves right or are doing it for fame
> -all parties agree to open with a show of respect speech
> --this speech contains
> Why we think they are a worthy opponent
> --what are are their major practices we would like to understand more
> --how we think those practices are important
> --how we think we could make them have a better experience with certain alternatives
> --why we think the concept is important and it will be a pleasure to have them as partners
>
> A Vimuktam Debate is not considered complete unless one party has "won the argument" by having been allowed by the other party to lead in a Vimuktam Things Project.
> Leadership necessarily with Partnership is the win in VD.
> A successful Vimuktam Debate is assessed by how much both parties felt matured, fulfilled and refined with their VT's experience and how much the "Leading team" or the winning team has been able to gain the respect and satisfaction of their followers or the "following team."
>
> Don't think a VD is not with its own tricks, and public speaking opportunities, you may lovingly cajole, berate and even insult sometimes, but only when a clear rapport and expressed respect has been established

Core concept: **Leadership necessarily with Partnership is the win in Vimuktam Debate.**

## PO-0021 — Happiness as voluntarily spent automation

> The time spent, voluntarily spending our Automation is called Happiness. It is not a person, a subject, an object of the senses or even an activity. Happiness is dependent yet different from all of these, it is a mental state born out of a combination of experiences in the present and various memories, and imaginations attached with such an experience. Sadness is also the exact same thing, accept we don't want to voluntarily experience it. The living being constantly wants to be happy, i.e. be able to spend their automation on something that makes them Happy. That is it. Everything that we are doing is just an expression of this one same desire.
>
> The law of diminishing returns not only proves this theory but also poses an alarming question on the nature of our consumption and production environment of the world. We are looking for happiness in earning and spending money (another way of saying automation) on material objects (objects that could easily become tools for Happiness) without the concept of Volunteering. We want to buy things for the self, without knowing who the self is. Thus creating momentary, elusive and illusionary happiness, the kind we think is coming from the object itself, everywhere in our interactions.
>
> Objects can give happiness when we have a relationship with the object. The "We" here is the real intrinsic soul, aware of itself, using the object (through automation) to enjoy such a relationship.
>
> This relationship comes when we have another soul to share our experience with such an object.
>
> Even if for a while someone may enjoy an object in the solitude of one's mind, no material objects can ever give happiness without it being shared with another soul.
> The only way to increase happiness coming from an object, is to increase love with the soul you are sharing it with. The ultimate state, of this love is when the bondage of the object and your relationship with it also breaks away and you only enjoy what your beloved other soul enjoys. You feel happiness, without a medium.
>
> This relationship first with a soul and because of the soul, with the object being shared is what turns bonded, involuntary work, into an act of Love. Increasing love is the only way in fact to become free of this circle of involuntary action. Developing such a relationship, with someone is the only way to cut through the law of diminishing returns. and Authentic Love seems to be the only exception to this law. The more we love someone, (I am not talking in vague romantic terms) the more happiness we get from an object. The more we want to collect it, without engaging with an object, without having anyone to share our experience of it with, without having someone to mirror it, the more boredom it creates.
>
> The purpose of this whole theory of Happiness is to explore the possibility of learning, how to create more and more happiness independent of all the activities. I.e. Not doing work to become happy, but being happy while working. This renders work as non-different from life. The most potent model for work-life balance Vimuktam, has a happy, an voluntarily available employee.
>
> Curiosity about some concept, and sharing it in company seems to be the simplest way to encourage people in a community to feel loved and valued. It is not at all and original idea. Trinkets of this wisdom are scattered all around us. But in the current corporate world, or rather the world that is corporate in nature, the very core of our impulse as living being, Happiness, has become a vague idea when it comes to workers, and a tool for sale, when it comes to consumers. In both cases this version of happiness is invariably bound by the law of diminishing returns.
>
> Vimuktam's concrete KPI's based concept of Happiness, becomes a tool of productivity for workers with reduced waste, errors and lethargy, and becomes a tool for directing consumption consciously and profitably amongst consumers without allowing it to spiral into frenzied buying and trashing of things.

This is one of the central Vimuktam theories of Happiness. Key terms: **Automation, Voluntary Action, Relationship, Curiosity, Love, Diminishing Returns, Consumption, Work, Happiness KPIs**.

## PO-0023 — Caste reservation and Vimuktam Reservations

> So there is a lot of confusion about caste based resrvation in india. I am not sure how to feel about it. Both sides make sense, the downtrodden deserves an equal chance, while the individuals in the general category must not be disadvantaged because of their birth.
>
> Vimuktam's answer neutral from both the critiques and advocates of caste based reservation
>
> -acknowledgement of caste based injustice, let's not deny the past injustice simply because we are upset with the current available "resolution" of it.
> -acknowledgement of disadvantages faced by general category, while the injustice is real, not all individuals have to be punished for the deeds of their previous generation
> -acknowledgement of the limitations of the system, many people who have been the beneficiaries of the caste based reservation simply are not qualified in terms of merit when it comes to do many jobs, to be maths teacher you must study maths, if you were disatvanged at a young age, be oming a teacher without studying is not equal opportunity, it is fighting inequality with more inequality
>
> The Vimuktam Reservations
>
> These are educational institutions reserved entirely for the historically underserved communities, SC's and ST's, that give them all the moral, psychological and educational boost they may have missed because of their caste etc. the institution is designed to bridge the gap between the two categories in terms of caste based issues such as dignity, representation, self-expression, ethnocentric pride, social etiquettes, trends etc. And then allows such individuals to compete with the rest of the society on equal platforms of merit. Instead of assuming that an SC/ST candidate lacks the same intelligence as the general category candidate, for which we look at a lower cut off etc. we provide the environment for an SC/ST aspirant first to come at the same level of education as their general category counterparts and then appear for the same entrance exams for example

The proposed model aims to distinguish **equal opportunity from merely equalized thresholds**, with preparation and institutional support preceding common merit-based competition.

## PO-0026 — Civilization versus culture: overarching philosophy

> So, the difference that I am perceiving now between a civilization and a culture is depending on the availability of some kind of an umbrella philosophy, and whether all the day-to-day work has that philosophy supporting it in some way or the other. To clarify this with an example, when we eat in India with our hands, you know, the food, there is a story to it in various cultures within the Indian subcontinent. Eating with your hands is explained through some story. Funnily, all of them may do it differently. Like, the Maharashtrians will give you your roti, they'll break it into parts and offer it to you when they are serving you food. That kind of shows love, it kind of shows a sense of belonging, a sense of family, etc. So there is a very clear progression of that action into a story, a philosophy of some kind, which will then be supported by some rishi, some great saint, etc., which will in turn incessantly, as if, like the River Ganges, pours itself into an overarching philosophical network of ideas that is just spread all over the country.
>
> ...there are various variations to this, and yet it all somehow is connected to the larger network of philosophies, also disagreeing with each other at the same time, and being a part of the intermingling network of thoughts.
>

- Conscious civilization as a civilization that remembers the experiencer.

## Contemplation / Mind

- Vimuktam as the **Science of Contemplation**.
- Planning, Acting, Being Still in the Now.
- Stillness as the foundational gear through which the other gears become governable.
- Intelligence as a tool that can choose to still the mind.
- **Discipline is the ability to return.**

## Practice

- Any idea that can be engaged with is worthy of Practice/debate.
- Practice can turn abstract questions into concrete experiments.
- Establish Dharma through Practice.
- Ideas should be evaluated through their consequences in Practice.

## Srishti–Sthiti–Laya

- The underlying three-part Vimuktam framework.
- Other techniques and concepts should be located within this framework rather than replacing it.

## Happiness

- Happiness as a mental state associated with voluntarily spent “automation.”
- Relationship, Curiosity and SEVA as a proposed Occam's razor for Happiness.
- Diminishing returns and conscious consumption.
- Happiness while working rather than work merely to obtain happiness.
- Happiness as a possible organizational KPI domain.

## Relationship / Bhakti

- Relationship as the prana/centre of Bhakti.
- Relationship as a source of Happiness.
- The contrast and complementarity of institutional discipline and spontaneous Brijwasi devotional expression.

## Debate

- Vimuktam Debate versus adversarial/Oxford/Macaulayan debate.
- “Interesting and complex” rather than “decided and boring.”
- Respect before rhetoric.
- Leadership with Partnership as the win.
- Debate completed through a useful project/Practice, not merely a declared winner.

## Dharma / Adharma

- Dharma centered on the soul.
- Dharma must be established repeatedly in each age.
- Dharma is subtle; Adharma is often loud.
- The present material-centered civilization is treated as the current context of Vimuktam's Dharmic work.
- Vimuktam's task is not rejection of modernity but establishment of Dharma within modernity.

## Civilization / Culture

- Culture as lived expression/custom.
- Civilization as an overarching network of ideas, narratives, institutions and practices.
- Civilization should not be defined only by buildings, factories or urban density.
- The civilizational question: **what idea sits at the centre?**
- Proposed Vimuktam answer: the conscious being.

## Institutions / Individuals

- Systems and rules matter, but individual decisions ultimately animate them.
- Institutional development should proceed through development of individuals.
- Brand is total lived experience, not merely customer perception.
- **Vimuktam is not just what the customers see, Vimuktam is.**

## Education / Development

- Happiness, voluntary engagement, curiosity and Practice as institutional development tools.
- Punishment versus curing the miscreant mind.
- Reservation model based on bridging educational/social disadvantage before common merit competition.

## Consumption / Industry

- Conscious consumption rather than blanket anti-consumption.
- Relationship with objects can reduce novelty-seeking and waste.
- Industry can be conscious.
- Consumption can be conscious.
- Technology can be conscious.
- Conflict can be conscious.
- Civilization can be conscious.

## Tantra / Ritual / Behaviour

- Rituals as behavioural and attentional technologies at minimum.
- Rituals are not *only* tools; their instrumental value is a minimum case.
- Mantra, shuchita, sanyam and niyam as disciplines that can transform behaviour and observation.
- The deeper question: whether such practices reveal knowledge or generate it through disciplined engagement.
